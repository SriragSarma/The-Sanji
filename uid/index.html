About The Sanji

Our Story

The Sanji is inspired from a pirate named Black Leg Sanji who had bounty over a billion berries from The Great Pirate Era. From his passion towards cooking and his love for women made him find the All Blue and The Chef of the King of the Pirates. This website is made for ladies to access their favorite recipes and for the students who are far from home.

The makers of the website — Srirag Sarma, Tapasya, and Charan — who collaborated to design and build this website "The Sanji" as part of our shared interest. Our goal was to create a website that can reach out to people who came far away from their home to pursue their career but faltered to due their neglection on health. The nostalgia they get from their home hits harder. Observing the life of these people inspired us to make the website that could reach out to people who can cook whenever and wherever they want breaking the barriers of the internet. This is the inspiration that made "The Sanji".
